Our Legacy

Infrastructure Projects

Bamburi Cement has been the trusted partner for East Africa's most ambitious infrastructure developments.

Road Infrastructure

Dongo Kundu Bypass

The 17.5km Mombasa bypass features three major bridges. Bamburi supplied over 50,000 tonnes of specialised concrete for marine-grade durability and heavy-load performance.

Railway Infrastructure

Standard Gauge Railway (SGR)

Bamburi supplied high-performance concrete for railway sleepers, bridge structures, and station platforms across the entire Mombasa–Nairobi corridor.

Sports Infrastructure

Talanta Sports Stadium

A FIFA-standard 60,000-seat stadium under construction. Bamburi's high-strength concrete is used for foundation works, terraces, and structural columns.

Bridge Construction

Makupa Bridge

A critical link between Mombasa Island and the mainland. Bamburi supplied specialised marine-grade concrete resistant to saltwater corrosion.

Water Infrastructure

Thwake Dam

Multi-purpose dam for water supply and hydropower. Bamburi's low-heat cement was formulated to prevent thermal cracking in this massive concrete gravity dam.

Water Infrastructure

Mwache Dam

A strategic water supply project for Mombasa and Kwale counties. Bamburi provided over 100,000 tonnes of specialised concrete for the dam structure and spillway.

Why Choose Us

Why Bamburi Cement?

Trusted by engineers, preferred by builders, certified for excellence.

70+ Years of Excellence

Since 1951, Bamburi Cement has been the foundation of East Africa's growth. Our legacy of quality and reliability spans over seven decades.

ISO & KEBS Certified

ISO 9001:2015 certified for quality management and ISO 14001:2015 for environmental management. Every bag meets Kenya Bureau of Standards (KEBS) specifications.

Sustainable Manufacturing

We've reduced CO₂ emissions by 25% through alternative fuels and green clinker technology. Our Eco-label products use up to 40% less carbon.

State-of-the-Art R&D

Our modern research laboratory in Mombasa develops innovative concrete solutions — from self-compacting concrete to high-durability marine mixes.

Technical Advisory Team

Access to a dedicated team of civil engineers and materials scientists providing on-site technical support, mix design optimisation, and quality assurance.

Pan-African Presence

Member of Holcim Group — the world's largest cement manufacturer — serving Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Burundi, and South Sudan.

Listed Company

Bamburi Cement is publicly traded on the Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE: BAMB), ensuring transparency, governance, and stakeholder accountability.

Community Impact

Through the Bamburi Foundation, we invest in education, healthcare, and environmental conservation — including the Bamburi Nature Trail and coral reef restoration.
